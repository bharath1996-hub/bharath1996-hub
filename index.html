Areas of Focus

Research Interests

Exploring the intersection of quantum computing, quantum chemistry, and machine learning to solve challenging problems in molecular simulation and materials science.

🔬

NISQ Algorithms

Developing near-term quantum algorithms for molecular systems using variational methods and Trotterization on IBM Heron quantum processors. Focus on NMR spectroscopy simulation and quantum dynamics.

VQE Trotterization IBM Quantum
🧠

Neural Quantum States

Implementing neural network-based wavefunctions using NetKet for time-dependent variational Monte Carlo. Applications to strongly coupled spin systems and interpretability analysis using Restricted Boltzmann Machines.

NetKet RBM t-VMC
⚡

Block Encoding & QSP

Researching fault-tolerant approaches for Hamiltonian simulation using Block Encoding and Quantum Signal Processing. Developing resource-efficient algorithms with symmetry-adapted circuits.

QSVT LCU Hamiltonian Simulation
🔮

Quantum Chemistry

Applying quantum algorithms to molecular systems including singlet fission dynamics for solar cells and CO₂ capture using Metal-Organic Frameworks. Focus on strongly correlated systems requiring quantum chemistry precision.

Molecular Simulation Materials Science Energy Applications
🎯

Quantum Many-Body Physics

Studying Heisenberg spin chains and many-body systems using subspace diagonalization methods. Expertise in connecting classical and quantum approaches to strongly coupled systems.

Spin Systems Subspace Methods Condensed Matter
🔧

Quantum Control

Exploring quantum control theory and pulse-level optimization, connecting variational algorithm experience to quantum hardware control challenges for improved algorithm implementation.

Pulse Optimization Hardware Control Error Mitigation
